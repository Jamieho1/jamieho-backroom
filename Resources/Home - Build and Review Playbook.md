## Scene acceptance checklist

- Opening originals retain pose, colour, clouds, bot shape and detail. Ambient figures stay rigid; Eye capsules hang upright; ship paths are plausible; no pointer-driven movement or strobe.
- Imperial looks recognisable. Library remains imagined at ground level with the required lawn details and no invented classical facade. Notebook/cup are fixed, absent at entry/exit and visible only during the middle passage.
- Westminster keeps the same geography and weather throughout the approach. No sudden bridge/model/photo substitution. The camera actually advances through depth and the underpass cover is authored.
- St Paul's retains the distant glass-building composition, black left/white text and vertical reveal/descent/black exit. Nearby floors pass upward plausibly, without excessive slabs blocking the whole view.
- Canary Wharf has the purple Elizabeth line roundel and reference-correct platform. Wall reveal replaces stairs. Side boarding, carriage materials and doorway relationships stay coherent.
- Room matches the detailed material/light/object brief. Arrival is angled and monitor approach resolves straight-on. Monitor text/forms are real and usable, not a texture, fake screenshot or false success state.
- Source and final pixels are sharp at stops. Native 24/30fps footage does not gain new motion frames from a 60fps UI loop. Measure the render/decode behaviour on named test conditions instead of promising universal 60fps or new 4K source detail.
