## Motion and sharpness quality bar — proposed implementation acceptance criteria

Jamie supplied https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgRGSOGNPM and asked for smooth, clear scroll parallax that can stop at an intermediate state and cleanly cross transitions. This adds a quality requirement; it does not authorize implementation or generation.

### Current source observations

- The existing desktop wallpaper has a fixed camera and clock-driven ambient effects, not a scroll-controlled camera/world.
- `components/desktop-scene/runtime.js` currently limits draws using 1000/30 and caps pixel ratio at 1.5 desktop / 1.25 small screen. These can constrain motion pacing and high-density sharpness; they are observations, not a measured performance diagnosis.
- The approved original rasters are 1672×941. A higher drawing-buffer resolution can sharpen native geometry/text/edges but cannot invent additional source image detail. Limit the original art's magnification; any higher-resolution derivative would require a separate fidelity review. No upscale is performed here.

### Required behavior

1. **Scroll controls the scene state.** Camera/layer progress follows scroll position in both directions, with a small bounded catch-up if useful. No forced chapter snapping or one-shot animation that keeps finishing while the user stops. Ambient character/weather movement can continue on its own clock without moving the paused camera. Clicking app navigation remains an intentional short room/monitor animation, distinct from scroll scrubbing.
2. **Depth uses coherent planes/geometry.** Background, midground, foreground and atmosphere have controlled depth; the original figure/bot shapes remain rigid. Do not create motion by warping or compressing the picture. Camera/path/perspective and pacing stay coherent through scene connections.
3. **Clarity is budgeted at the target display.** Match the canvas drawing buffer to the intended display density within a measured GPU budget. Avoid CSS stretching a low-resolution canvas or hiding joins with full-scene blur. Keep text/forms as real UI. Reduce expensive particles/shadows/post effects before sacrificing the whole picture's clarity; choose stable quality settings rather than visibly pumping resolution while scrolling.
4. **Asset/scene continuity holds at the join.** Keep one visual stage with stable framing and a shared progress coordinator. Adjacent scenes/clip frames are ready and compositionally matched before visibility changes. No black/poster flash, conflicting crops, double figures, bright alpha gaps or unintentional blur. Any film/sequence chosen must be encoded/loaded for interactive seeking and actually tested for reverse/hold behavior.
5. **Frame pacing is measured.** Target stable 60 fps for the live-camera/UI proof on a named desktop test device where the scene budget allows, then profile actual frame times. This is not a universal-device guarantee or a claim that a 24/30 fps source clip gains new frames from a 60 fps render loop. Record source frame rate/resolution separately if clips are used.
6. **Every intermediate state is valid.** Evaluate slow forward travel, fast travel, reversal and holds inside each scene/transition, including 25%, 50% and 75% positions. Paused camera frames should stay composed and sharp; resumed travel should not jump. Test theme changes during/near joins and room app navigation on cold/warm assets.
7. **Proof precedes full production.** The first later technical prototype includes ONE representative scroll connection AND the room/monitor interaction. Compare a live 3D/layered approach with a seekable-film/sequence approach only where needed; select by actual clarity, control and loading behavior. Actual interactive rendering/pausing is the evidence, not merely an exported video or a transform assertion.
8. **Phone and fallback behavior is intentional.** Test a separate 390 px composition and reduced-motion/no-WebGL/no-JS routes. Real-device playback/scrolling is reviewed by Jamie; local headless checks do not certify every phone decoder or GPU. Clear functional forms/pages remain available.

Primary technical references: [GSAP ScrollTrigger scrub/snap](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/) and [Three.js display/drawing-buffer sizing](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/responsive.html). These support the control/resolution mechanics; the target values and quality gates above are proposed project criteria, not guarantees from a library.
