## Acceptance evidence for the interactive stage

- Slow/fast scroll, stop and reverse across the library, full-cover point and river reveal. Check meaningful intermediate holds (including quarter/half/three-quarter transition positions).
- Rigid shapes and stable perspective: no desk/cup/tower/architecture warping; no pointer-driven camera/figure response.
- Native readable HTML and crisp render buffer within a measured budget. Record source size/frame rate separately; aim for stable desktop 60 fps where the scene budget permits, without claiming universal hardware performance.
- Correct reference orientation and stable exposure; no black/poster/loading/blur flash, forced auto-finish or hard crop change.
- Monitor/direct page/close interactions, keyboard use, normal content scrolling, reduced-motion/unavailable-graphics behavior and 390 px layout without horizontal overflow.
- Local checks and measured/visual evidence, followed by Jamie's review. An exported movie, still image or transform assertion alone does not prove interactive quality.
