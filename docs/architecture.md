Content hub data model (H1, migration `20261125100000_content_hub.sql`, applied live 2026-11-25 as remote version 20261125130615; Latest/shell is H2, series, guides and the post page are H3-H5): `public.content_series` (slug, title ≤80, goal ≤200 = the "After this:" line, topic = the article category enum, planned_parts 1-50, sort, published) is ordered runs of posts and guides. `articles` gain series_id, series_part (1-50), code (unique, ≤16 uppercase letters/digits, nullable, the post code from the last slide), takeaway (≤160, the "You'll learn" line) and slides (≤20 ordered HTTPS image URLs); `guides` gain series_id, series_part, audience (≤200), highlights (≤3 × ≤80) and exercises (nullable int 0-999). Series membership is both-or-neither, and a part number is used once per series across articles AND guides (trigger with an advisory lock); deleting a series detaches its members. Read time (`readMinutes`, words/220 rounded up, min 1) and chapter count (`chapterCount`, "## " headings) are computed from the body, never stored. Validation is shared zod: `lib/articles.ts` (articleSchema/articleObject, slugSchema, seriesPartSchema), `lib/guides.ts`, new `lib/content-series-data.ts` (seriesSchema, selects, seriesHref); `lib/content-series.ts` stays the old topic label map. Public detail reads (`getPublicArticle/Guide`), the owner editors and saves all select every column (ARTICLE_SELECT/GUIDE_SELECT); the old ARTICLE_PUBLIC_SELECT/GUIDE_PUBLIC_SELECT are gone.

Styling: app/content/content.css on the My story tokens with day and night themes. /content/series and /content/guides are plain placeholders until H3/H4; series search results link to /content/series until the series pages exist. YouTube cards say "N min video" from the body's read time (no video length is stored).
